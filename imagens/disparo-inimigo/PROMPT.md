## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE isolated 2D game item for Oficina em Pane. Image 1 is only a style reference for thick warm-black hand-inked 1930s cartoon outlines, painted cel shading, old factory metal and readable game items. Do not copy the whole scene. Clean genuinely transparent background, generous padding, no text, no labels, no UI, no floor, no shadows, no watermark, no characters. Simple clear silhouette readable when reduced to a small sprite. A single horizontal enemy projectile pointing RIGHT, a compact ROUND RED-HOT METAL PELLET with a dark crimson thick outline, red outer body, small warm pale-red central highlight, and two short triangular red motion fins/trails to its LEFT. Shape should be visibly rounder and chunkier than an elongated player bolt, so dangerous enemy shots differ by SHAPE as well as color. No gold/cream long teardrop, no gun, huge explosion or smoke cloud. Simple old-factory cartoon threat, not futuristic laser.
