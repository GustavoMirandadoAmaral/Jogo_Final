## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE isolated 2D game item for Oficina em Pane. Image 1 is only a style reference for thick warm-black hand-inked 1930s cartoon outlines, painted cel shading, old factory metal and readable game items. Do not copy the whole scene. Clean genuinely transparent background, generous padding, no text, no labels, no UI, no floor, no shadows, no watermark, no characters. Simple clear silhouette readable when reduced to a small sprite. A single horizontal projectile pointing RIGHT, a compact elongated ivory-white hot steam-pressure bolt with a warm amber/orange outline and a short tapered curved orange tail at its LEFT. Rounded pointed nose, clean continuous teardrop silhouette, only a couple simple flat cel-painted highlights. Friendly warm cream/amber, not red. No gun, casing, huge explosion, long smoke cloud or scattered sparks. All parts fit within padding. The visible projectile itself is the item.
