## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset for the 2D side-view platform game Oficina em Pane. Match the approved factory's hand-inked 1930s cartoon linework, worn industrial iron, rivets, copper pipes, warm furnace light and muted blue-gray distant architecture. It is a steam-powered old factory. Strict side-elevation art, no isometric floor, no characters, no enemies, pickups, bullets, interface, labels, words, watermark or logo. Any screenshots are references for the artwork only: ignore and REMOVE all app text, black screenshot margins, cropping handles and rounded screenshot frames. Create exactly ONE independent game layer per image. Image 1 references the exact desired RIGHT-EDGE workshop assembly; image 2 supplies the original style. Create a single tall PORTRAIT right-border foreground cutout, about 2:3 aspect ratio. The visual idea is the tall riveted black iron lattice support and vertical copper piping, with a big orange-brown boiler and warm furnace window in the lower RIGHT area, round valve wheel, pipes and metal scaffold at the bottom, and a small hanging old lamp in the upper area. Machinery is concentrated along the RIGHT side, with the inner LEFT edge irregular and transparent around pipes, girders and steam. Preserve visibly open transparent gaps through the support lattice. The outer right and lower machinery may meet their canvas edges naturally. Extract/redraw only nearer machinery; remove the blue-gray workshop wall, factory windows, distant machines, any opaque rectangular panel and all screenshot UI. TRUE TRANSPARENT ALPHA wherever no foreground machinery exists. Keep steam localized. This must be a right boundary piece ONLY, not a full frame, not a second left boiler or entire background room.
