## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset for the 2D side-view platform game Oficina em Pane. Match the approved factory's hand-inked 1930s cartoon linework, worn industrial iron, rivets, copper pipes, warm furnace light and muted blue-gray distant architecture. It is a steam-powered old factory. Strict side-elevation art, no isometric floor, no characters, no enemies, pickups, bullets, interface, labels, words, watermark or logo. Any screenshots are references for the artwork only: ignore and REMOVE all app text, black screenshot margins, cropping handles and rounded screenshot frames. Create exactly ONE independent game layer per image. Create the FAR BACKGROUND layer only: a wide horizontal panorama about 2.5:1 of the rear interior architecture of this same enormous factory. An opaque complete blue-gray softly painted wall fills the canvas: very tall dusty arched factory windows, slender distant riveted roof trusses, high horizontal catwalk silhouettes and faint large flywheel silhouettes far in the distance. Diffuse pale daylight, restrained tiny amber lamps if needed. Low contrast, muted desaturated blue-gray, softly atmospheric 1930s painted cartoon backdrop. Leave broad calm areas in the middle and lower thirds so machinery and sprites can be placed over it. NO large foreground boiler, NO close-up pipes, NO heavy border columns at the left or right edge, NO hanging chains near the viewer, NO playable foreground floor or floating platforms. It should feel like the rear wall and depth of the factory, not a finished scene containing the other layers. Clean continuous panorama, no picture border, no black bars.
