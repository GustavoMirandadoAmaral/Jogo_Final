## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: SECOND HORIZONTAL SECTION of a transparent middle-depth parallax factory layer for Oficina em Pane.
Image 1: the existing "boilers and pipes" middle-depth panel. This is the exact art style, depth, scale, bottom alignment and side-by-side continuity reference.
Image 2: the distant factory window layer. It will remain behind BOTH middle-depth panels; do NOT paint its wall or windows into the new panel.
Primary request: generate ONE new wide panorama of an old steam-powered ASSEMBLY CONVEYOR LINE. It is intended to be placed immediately to the RIGHT of image 1, so the player sees the factory change naturally from boiler machinery to an assembly sector as the camera travels.
Intended canvas 1983x793 pixels, matching image 1's wide 2.5:1 proportions. Keep the same physical machinery scale, bottom edge alignment, side-elevation camera, warm hand-inked dark contours, painted 1930s cartoon rendering, muted graphite iron, worn copper/brown, rivets and restrained orange lights as image 1. Nothing is photorealistic or 3D.
At the FAR LEFT of the new panel, continue the industrial pipe/catwalk/boiler-support vocabulary of image 1's RIGHT edge: a partial copper boiler side, flanged pipe elbow and iron catwalk rail at similar heights. Transition smoothly into a LONG clearly recognizable assembly conveyor occupying the middle and lower area of the new panel. Keep connecting pipe/catwalk heights visually compatible, not a hard wall at the join.
The production line has a horizontal metal slat/rubberized dark conveyor belt, visible round rollers and large side drive wheel, riveted support legs, old mechanical belt transmission, several partly assembled empty steel shells and simple gear blanks on the belt. Two sturdy pneumatic piston press stations or mechanical stamping tools stand over different portions of the conveyor, fed by copper pipes and driven by a small side steam engine with valves and analog pressure gauges. Factory technology of the early industrial period: no electronic panels, lasers, modern robot arms or screens. A few small warm utility lamps and localized translucent steam puffs only. Parts are inert unfinished metal workpieces, not characters or collectible XP.
Layout: machinery occupies mostly the LOWER 60 to 70 percent, with irregular silhouettes rising to varied heights. Upper 25 to 30 percent must remain entirely TRANSPARENT. All openings between press supports, beneath the belt and between pipes must also be transparent to show the distant window background through them. No enclosing side borders, no opaque wall, no painted room, no sky, no solid haze across the canvas. The lower machinery may meet the bottom edge naturally, matching the other panel. No foreground walkable floor, gameplay platforms, players, enemies, workers, UI, bullets, title, labels, symbols or watermark.
This is BACKGROUND DECORATION, not a playable conveyor platform. One continuous coherent factory section, not a collage or catalog of props. TRUE TRANSPARENT ALPHA background. The production line should be readable and simpler than foreground characters. Preserve enough open space for the distant factory architecture to be visible.
