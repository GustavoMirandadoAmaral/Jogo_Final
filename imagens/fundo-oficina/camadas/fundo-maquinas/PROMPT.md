## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset for the 2D side-view platform game Oficina em Pane. Match the approved factory's hand-inked 1930s cartoon linework, worn industrial iron, rivets, copper pipes, warm furnace light and muted blue-gray distant architecture. It is a steam-powered old factory. Strict side-elevation art, no isometric floor, no characters, no enemies, pickups, bullets, interface, labels, words, watermark or logo. Any screenshots are references for the artwork only: ignore and REMOVE all app text, black screenshot margins, cropping handles and rounded screenshot frames. Create exactly ONE independent game layer per image. Create ONLY the independent MIDDLE-DEPTH machinery layer as a wide horizontal panorama about 2.5:1. A coherent row of different old boilers, copper pipe elbows, pressure valves, low iron catwalks, and two large flywheels occupies the LOWER 50 to 60 percent of the canvas, with different machine heights and OPEN transparent gaps between structures. The upper 35 percent and openings between machinery must be TRUE TRANSPARENT ALPHA so the far factory windows remain visible behind this layer. No opaque wall or sky at all. Machines are seen from the side at intermediate distance; use muted warm brown/graphite metal and restrained orange furnace windows, less saturated and lower contrast than the large foreground edge boilers. Vary small and medium boilers across the width, not two massive close-up border assemblies. No giant enclosing frame, no decorated top border. Steam may be small and localized with soft transparency, not a solid haze sheet. The machines are background decoration, so do not add a crisp walkable floor strip, foreground platforms or collectible objects. Keep central shapes simple enough for foreground gameplay readability. This is one connected scene layer, not individual props arranged like a catalog.
