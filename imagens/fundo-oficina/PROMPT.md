## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: panoramic workshop background CONCEPT for the 2D side-view platform game Oficina em Pane.
Image 1: approved gameplay screen; use ONLY its factory architecture, hand-painted cartoon rendering and muted industrial palette as the style/environment reference. Remove every gameplay character, enemy, UI element, projectile, collectible and floating playable platform from the design.
Primary request: a beautiful wide panorama of the inside of a vast old steam-powered workshop, late-19th/early-20th-century large factory technology imagined in a 1930s theatrical cartoon. This will guide the background layers for a horizontally scrolling arena.
Composition: panoramic landscape about 2.5:1, edge-to-edge background environment, strict side-elevation illustration rather than isometric, no converging perspective floor. Show varied connected bays of the same continuous factory with a coherent horizontal structure. Tall dusty arched factory windows, riveted roof trusses and steel columns, copper pipes and pressure valves, old boilers with a little warm furnace light, gear and flywheel silhouettes, hanging lamps, modest steam haze. No text.
Depth hierarchy: distant windows and roof structure in faded desaturated blue-gray; mid-distance stationary machinery and pipes in muted warm gray/brown/copper; foreground game characters will be placed over the image later, so keep the central play area airy and low contrast, especially the middle and lower thirds. Dark structural supports may frame the extreme edges but must not block the middle.
This is BACKGROUND DECOR only. No walkable foreground floor strip, conveyor gameplay terrain, floating shelves/platforms, ladders, hazards, oil pools or foreground clutter. Machinery near the bottom may fade into soft darker silhouettes. No robots, no living characters, no pickups, no projectiles, no reticle, no UI, no title, no labels, no border.
Style: match the approved gameplay reference's expressive warm dark ink linework and hand-painted old-factory atmosphere, watercolor/cel-painted background texture, muted teal-gray and brown with restrained ivory lamp glow and copper warmth. Background contrast and saturation must remain lower than the approved hero so combat sprites and golden XP will be clearly readable. Slight vintage paint imperfection, no cinematic bloom, no photorealism, no 3D render.
It is a single complete background panorama for concept validation, not a cutaway, split-panel diagram, collage, or a sheet of separately labeled layers. No black bars, watermarks, logos or modern electronics.
