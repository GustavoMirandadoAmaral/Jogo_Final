## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE isolated 2D game item for Oficina em Pane. Image 1 is only a style reference for thick warm-black hand-inked 1930s cartoon outlines, painted cel shading, old factory metal and readable game items. Do not copy the whole scene. Clean genuinely transparent background, generous padding, no text, no labels, no UI, no floor, no shadows, no watermark, no characters. Simple clear silhouette readable when reduced to a small sprite. A single small collectible golden BRASS GEAR, face-on view, eight broad rounded teeth, circular central hole visibly transparent, beveled warm gold metal rim with simple ivory cel highlights. Slight old-workshop patina but bright and inviting. No coin face or currency symbols, no sparkle stars floating outside the object. Gold identifies collectible XP.
