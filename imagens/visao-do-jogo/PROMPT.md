# Oficina em Pane: conceito de uma partida v1

Imagem: `oficina-em-pane-gameplay-conceito-v1.png`.

Visualização conceitual gerada por IA com a ferramenta de imagens integrada ao Codex. Usa o herói-caldeira aprovado como referência e representa a arena durante uma onda, conforme a descrição do GDD. É uma proposta visual para avaliar a direção de arte e a leitura da jogabilidade; não é uma captura de uma implementação em execução. Os valores e posições da interface são ilustrativos.

Referência do protagonista: [robô-caldeira aprovado](../heroi/heroi-caldeira-conceito-v1.png).

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: high quality widescreen 16:9 gameplay screen concept for the original 2D platform survival shooter "Oficina em Pane".
Input image 1: APPROVED PLAYER CHARACTER IDENTITY. Preserve this exact little steam robot: rounded aged ivory riveted boiler head, round orange furnace porthole WITHOUT a face, crooked copper exhaust with a small steam puff, charcoal hose limbs, round brass torso pressure gauge and valve, oversized work boots, metal clamp left hand, pneumatic cannon right arm. Adapt his pose and scale to gameplay. The character must stay recognizable and must NOT become a television robot.
Primary request: show what a real playable mid-run screen might look like, with coherent mechanics and readable platforms, rather than a promotional poster. One continuous active gameplay view, no montage, no split panels, no upgrade selection overlay, no title screen. The camera is a strict SIDE-ON 2D platform view. Horizontal walkable surfaces and gravity; no isometric or top-down perspective.
Scene: interior of a vast early-20th-century industrial workshop, drawn as a charming but dangerous 1930s hand-inked theatrical cartoon. Riveted steel beams, large arched factory windows high above, distant stationary gears, boilers, copper pipes, conveyor machinery, pressure gauges, subtle steam haze. Painted background layers fade into muted desaturated gray-teal and warm brown, leaving the gameplay foreground distinct. All readable terrain is in a single side-view plane.
Arena layout: broad lower ground floor around 85% of image height, a clearly recognizable short conveyor section, and THREE separated floating industrial shelf/scaffold platforms at staggered jumpable heights in the central play area. Space beneath shelves is open so jumping upward through them is plausible. Two higher shelves offer refuge from ground walkers; one right-hand scaffold has a cracked support and a few falling bolts to suggest it can collapse. Leave broad traversal gaps and clean negative space, no maze. A small black-red oil leak pools in a corner of the ground floor; do not flood the collectible area during this mid-run wave.
Action: only ONE player, standing near the left-center on the lowest raised shelf. Player height is about 11 percent of image height, not a giant foreground illustration. His cannon is aimed diagonally right and upward toward a flying enemy, with a small muzzle flash and 2-3 warm cream-orange projectiles following a straight line. Put a small visible aiming reticle near the intended target so manual mouse aiming is communicated. The player's head, feet, cannon and supporting platform are clearly legible.
Enemies: a visible small HORDE converges from both ground edges, in clear readable spacing. They are entirely mechanical NON-HUMANOID industrial automatons, smaller than or comparable to the hero except the heavy. Several graphite-gray low wheeled walkers occupy the ground; a green spring-legged hopper is leaping toward a shelf; two blue spherical propeller drones threaten the upper space; one orange broad boxy tracked heavy threatens a platform support; one purple short stationary turret with a round lens sits on the far right, firing only a few clearly RED enemy projectiles. About 10-12 visible enemies, avoid filling every gap. Keep colors slightly aged/desaturated but recognizable, each type with a distinct silhouette. No enemy resembles a second player.
Core mechanic must be visible: numerous small GOLDEN NUTS AND GEARS (experience pickups, not coins or gems) lie along the dangerous lower floor among the walkers. Platforms have no experience pickups. Player can shelter higher up but must descend toward ground enemies to collect parts and evolve. Keep pickups visibly separate from bullets.
HUD: compact industrial enamel/brass panels tucked into the screen edges, never obscuring the central action. Top-left panel: EXACT readable Portuguese text "VIDA" with a red health bar showing roughly 70 percent remaining and a small boiler-robot portrait. Top-center: EXACT "ONDA 4" and smaller timer "03:28". Top-right: EXACT "NÍVEL 5" and a gold experience bar labeled EXACT "XP". Bottom-left, a very small row of three installed-part pictograms (reinforced cannon, gear, magnet), and a subtle dash cooldown indicator. No other words anywhere. Attractive simple vintage lettering, readable at gameplay size. HUD should occupy less than 12 percent of screen height.
Style: faithful to the approved robot's hand-drawn cartoon rendering; expressive warm-black ink contours, curved rubber-hose mechanical limbs, readable cel-shaded characters, painted old-factory background, muted ivory/iron/copper palette with restrained golden XP, red danger, green/blue/orange/purple enemy coding. Light vintage paper/film character in the BACKGROUND only, do not dirty the HUD or blur sprites. Factory architecture can be rich but keep the playable layer simpler and brighter than the background.
Constraints: actual 2D side-view game composition, landscape 16:9, crisp professional concept art, strong visual hierarchy. No boss present during this mid-run wave, no boss health bar, no cinematic closeup, no perspective floor, no 3D render, no realistic humans, no giant player, no extra player copies, no modern electronics, no existing game characters, no watermarks, no developer editor UI.
