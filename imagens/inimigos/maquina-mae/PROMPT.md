## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: INDIVIDUAL ENEMY CHARACTER CONCEPT for the original 2D platform game Oficina em Pane, intended for a student team review.
Input image 1: STYLE and material reference only, the approved protagonist boiler robot. Do not copy its humanoid body or turn it into an enemy.
Input image 2: approved game scene; reference for the factory world, enemy color coding and cartoon family.
Visual language: hand-inked 1930s theatrical cartoon meets early industrial steam machinery. Confident thick warm-black outlines, simple rounded readable shapes, restrained painted cel shading, worn enamel, cast iron, rivets, copper and brass pipes, analog gauges, warm amber inspection lenses or furnace ports, small steam details. Clearly mechanical, non-human, original designs. No photorealism or 3D render.
Deliver exactly ONE self-contained enemy, full object completely visible from all extremities with generous transparent margins of at least 6 percent; right-facing near-side three-quarter view suitable for a 2D platform game. A single clear silhouette readable at small size. Genuinely transparent alpha background. No floor, no cast ground shadow, no scenery, no extra character, no protagonist, no labels, no arrows, no text, no UI, no logos or watermark. No human face, eyes, teeth, mouth, clothing, modern electronic screen or futuristic armor. Do not include whole gameplay reference content.
Specific enemy design:
THE GIANT FINAL BOSS: an imposing self-contained MAIN STEAM ENGINE / FACTORY PRESS, rather than a humanoid robot. A massive central rounded rectangular riveted deep oxblood-red and graphite iron boiler housing, with a large circular orange-lit furnace inspection door and thick brass ring at its center, supported by four short wide cast-iron industrial support feet. Three vertical boiler/exhaust towers of different heights rise behind it, connected by thick copper pipes and valves, emitting restrained ivory steam. Two massive SIDE INDUSTRIAL PRESS ASSEMBLIES on horizontal piston rods end in blocky press heads or large mechanical grippers; they must look like factory mechanisms, not human arms or hands. One large analog pressure gauge and a few huge clear bolts, broad enamel panels and exposed mechanical joints. A commanding large fortress-like silhouette, wider than tall, with unmistakable weight and vintage factory character. The orange furnace suggests a potential central weak area without a human face. Conceptually several times larger than the hero, but do not draw the hero or a scale figure. Use a wide canvas with all chimneys, side presses, pipes and support feet FULLY in view and generous margins. This is an original proposed boss appearance; no attack diagrams, no extra characters, no boss UI, no floor, no scene background. Avoid a tank shape or legs resembling a person.
