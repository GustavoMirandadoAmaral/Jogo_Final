## Prompt da geração inicial

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: three-stage repair and evolution concept sheet for the original game hero of Oficina em Pane.
Image 1: the APPROVED steam boiler robot character; preserve its identity strictly.
Primary request: show exactly THREE versions of this same robot in a single wide horizontal composition, LEFT TO RIGHT: very rusty and battered but functional; partly repaired and cleaner; fully restored, polished and working perfectly. This evolution should express collecting scrap parts and XP to rebuild the same machine.
Invariant character design: aged ivory rounded riveted boiler head, SINGLE ROUND orange furnace inspection porthole without eyes or mouth, bent copper exhaust pipe, small steam puff, charcoal hose limbs, round brass chest pressure gauge with a needle, torso valve and copper connecting pipe, large rounded metal boots, broad clamp left hand, pneumatic cannon right forearm. Keep every design element recognizable and in the same location. NOT the earlier television/screen robot.
Alignment: all three FULL BODY figures at the same physical scale, same head proportions, same cannon length, same ground baseline, exactly the same relaxed right-facing three-quarter combat-ready stance, centered in three evenly spaced columns with generous margins. Only the state of wear and restoration changes, never the overall size or anatomy. No missing limbs.
Stage 1 at left: substantial reddish-brown iron rust, chipped ivory enamel, dents, one patched casing seam, worn copper, soot around the exhaust. Furnace still orange and the cannon still functional. Use a few large readable damage marks.
Stage 2 in center: much less rust, most large cracks repaired, visible replacement panel and repair seams, refreshed enamel but some patina. Same complete robot.
Stage 3 at right: fully repaired intact ivory enameled steel plates, cleaned graphite iron, polished copper/brass rings and pipes, a couple controlled bright metal highlights, intact gauge, healthy orange furnace, small confident steam puff. Absolutely no rust or broken plates. Still a vintage steam machine.
Style: match the approved reference illustration's thick warm-black hand-inked outlines, rounded cartoon forms, appealing weighted proportions, 1930s theatrical animation influence, restrained cel shading, metal/enamel materials; no photorealism or 3D render.
Background: genuine transparent alpha, no floor, no cast ground shadows, no solid background or checkerboard baked into pixels.
No text, arrows, captions, UI, icons, numbered panels, extra figures, extra poses, logos or watermark. This is a concept comparison, not an animation sheet.

Make ONLY two targeted corrections:
1. Recompose onto a roomier wide canvas, with ALL three figures reduced enough to fit completely within separate columns. Give visible transparent padding around every hand, cannon, boot and steam puff. No cannon, clamp, foot or smoke may touch or be cut by the image edges. Leave a clear transparent gap between the silhouettes.
2. The RIGHTMOST final-stage robot must look FULLY RESTORED AND POLISHED, brand-new restored vintage machinery. Remove ALL chipped paint, brown/gray damage patches, corrosion spots, rust, soot, dents, visible repair patches and scratches from its ivory head, chest, cannon and boot plates. Replace those marks with smooth intact creamy ivory enamel. Keep intentional dark structural seams, charcoal hose limbs, rivets and brass rings. Copper/brass should be clean and polished with a couple controlled cel-painted highlights. This remains hand-drawn cel-shaded metal, not photorealistic chrome or a 3D render.
Keep the LEFT robot substantially rusty and patched and the CENTER robot partially restored and slightly worn. Do not clean those two completely. Preserve the center's repair patch. Keep the right character's steam clean ivory.
All three remain the SAME approved mechanical hero: one round orange furnace porthole, no human face or eyes, bent exhaust, chest pressure gauge and valve, clamp and pneumatic cannon, boots.
Exactly three complete figures, no extra elements, no text or labels, no arrows, no ground shadows. Genuine transparent alpha background.
