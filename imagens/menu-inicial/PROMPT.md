## Prompt

Create a polished illustrated title-screen BACKGROUND for the 2D steampunk factory game Oficina em Pane. Use reference 1 as the exact world, architecture and rendering style reference: enormous old iron factory, blue-gray arched windows, huge gears, bronze pipes, orange furnace light, overhead beams, steam and a heavy metal floor. Use reference 2 as the character identity reference: friendly compact white/cream weathered boiler robot, single glowing orange circular eye, brass trim, chimney puff, claw hand and cannon arm, stout boots. New wide cinematic 16:9 composition. Put this one robot, full body, large in the RIGHT THIRD, standing on a steel factory platform facing slightly LEFT toward the empty menu area. Hero should occupy roughly 55 percent of image height, eye warm and luminous. Robot remains true to reference proportions and design. The left 44 percent of the screen must be dark navy charcoal with only subdued architectural forms, enough clean negative space for large title and a stack of buttons added later. The factory continues into depth in the upper middle and behind the robot, bright dusty orange pools of light with cool blue silhouettes. Ground at bottom, vertical pipe on far right. Rich hand-painted outlines matching the game art, controlled contrast, crafted game key art, atmospheric but readable. Leave all typography and user-interface out of the image: no text, no letters, no logo, no buttons, no frames, no watermark. Landscape wide 16:9 output.

## Placa de créditos

Referência visual em `prototipo/assets/botao_iniciar2.png`. Arquivo usado em `prototipo/imagens/menu/botao-creditos-v1.png`.

Edit this exact steampunk game button. Preserve its composition, hand-painted industrial art, ornate golden gear on left, bronze pipes, steam puffs, dark rusted metal nameplate, glowing amber slits on right, same proportions, same transparent background. Change the large embossed ivory word INICIAR to the Portuguese word CRÉDITOS, exactly including the acute accent on É. Replace the triangular play icon inside the left gear with a small brass open-book / team credits emblem, clear readable. Keep all decorative elements consistent with reference. No other text. Wide horizontal button, tightly framed, transparent PNG. This must match the existing game's INICIAR and SAIR buttons.

## Placa do título

Referência visual em `prototipo/assets/botao_iniciar2.png`. Arquivo usado em `prototipo/imagens/menu/titulo-v1.png`.

Create an illustrated game title nameplate using the supplied button as the EXACT visual style reference. Worn gunmetal and iron, rounded thick brass/gold bevel borders, copper pipes, mechanical gear details, rivets and two modest cream steam puffs. Embossed ivory serif lettering with warm amber highlights and deep dark outlines, same typography style as reference. The exact title is OFICINA EM PANE. Set the title on TWO LINES: top line OFICINA, bottom line EM PANE, centered, large and legible. The nameplate must be wider than tall, approximately 2.5:1 aspect, enough room for both lines, tightly framed with low transparent margins. A single crafted plaque/logo for a factory game menu, transparent background. No play icon, no buttons, no subtitle, no extra words. Match the bronze mechanical look of the reference, including bold readable cream letters and rusted charcoal plate.
