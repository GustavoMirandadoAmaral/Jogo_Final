Conceito original de robô industrial a vapor para Oficina em Pane, gerado por IA com a ferramenta de imagens integrada ao Codex. A imagem fornecida pelo usuário foi usada como referência de proporções e linguagem visual. Esta é uma proposta de aparência para avaliação, ainda não um sprite final ou uma folha de animação.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original playable robot hero concept for the 2D game Oficina em Pane.
Input image 1: reference ONLY for the appealing compact proportions, bold illustrated outlines and rounded mechanical shapes the user likes. Invent an entirely new character identity, not another revision of the orange CRT robot.
Primary request: one charming, unmistakably mechanical steam-powered factory robot hero, imagined by a 1930s rubber-hose animation studio. A small walking boiler built from cast iron, riveted enameled steel, copper pipes and pneumatic machinery. It should belong in an old enormous factory and feel agile enough to run and jump.
Design: oversized rounded rectangular cast-metal head with a SINGLE ROUND riveted inspection porthole glowing warm furnace-orange, visibly different from a television screen. No human face, no eyes, no mouth. Short bent exhaust pipe with a little puff of ivory steam instead of an electronic antenna. Compact rounded boiler torso with a small analog pressure gauge and one clearly visible brass valve. Flexible charcoal mechanical hose limbs, simple piston elbows and knees, large rounded metal work boots. One hand is a simple metal clamp/mitten; the other forearm is a broad, short pneumatic cannon with a brass pressure collar, barrel opening and one visible connecting steam pipe. Expressive springy stance and confident silhouette. Favor a few BIG memorable shapes over tiny mechanical clutter.
Style: hand-drawn 1930s theatrical cel cartoon, confident thick warm-black ink contours, pleasing flowing curves, playful rubber-hose motion implied by the pose, flat painted fills and restrained two-tone cel shadows, lightly worn enamel. Match the original reference's accessible cartoon appeal. Not a 3D render.
Palette: warm aged ivory enamel, graphite iron, muted copper/brass, warm orange furnace glow. Metal should read through rivets, panel seams and a few simple painted highlights rather than photorealistic reflections.
Composition: exactly ONE full-body character, head-to-toe visible, near-side three-quarter view facing right, cannon held ready, large on the canvas with clean margins. A tiny steam puff may rise from the head pipe. Genuinely transparent background and crisp cutout edges.
Avoid: text, labels, evolution stages, extra poses, background scene, platform, ground shadow, gears sprinkled everywhere, ornate fantasy steampunk costume, top hat, leather clothing, goggles, futuristic armor, LED screens, modern electronics, humanoid facial features, copied existing game character, bloom, smooth airbrush shading, logos, watermark.
